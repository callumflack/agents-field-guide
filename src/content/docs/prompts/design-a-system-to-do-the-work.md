> Someone I know asked Astra to play Factorio. It wins, but it takes forever and guzzles tokens. If you ask Astra instead to “design a system to win Factorio,” it builds software that runs 1000x faster.

The post gives no benchmark details. [@komorama’s original post](https://x.com/komorama/status/2120217991271084180).

Use this reframe when you want the agent to design a system for an outcome it’s pursuing step by step.

Give someone a fish and you feed them for a day. Teach them to fish and you feed them for a lifetime.
