```text
Before deciding on a direction, go read some books and essays to inspire yourself
```

The reading list shown in Stelzer’s screenshot:

- The singular continuous diffraction measure of the Thule-Morse chain
- Notes and Note-Pairs in Nørgård’s Infinity Series
- Oscillating Sequences (Feigenbaum attractor and the adding machine)
- Feigenbaum, The Transition to Aperiodic Behavior in Turbulent Systems
- Horvitz on Stockhausen’s “…how time passes…”
- Borges, Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius
- Döbereiner, Models of Constructed Sound
- Dichotic pitch demos (Stanford)
- Smyth and Hsu, SMC 2019 (time-varying poles)
- Wave Pulse Phase Modulation, DAFx 2025

Use when exploring ideas, before settling on an approach.
