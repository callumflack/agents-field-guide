---
title: Give a long task a worklog
description: Keep decisions and open questions that would otherwise disappear with the chat.
lastUpdated: 2026-11-12
---

Use a short `LOG.md` in the project when work may outlast the chat. Keep the goal, decisions made in conversation, open questions, and the next action. Update it when one of those changes.

I tried Eric Provencher’s worklog idea on a design-system project. The log started repeating project status I could already find elsewhere. The useful part was keeping the feedback I’d given in chat and the work it left open.

I still use a worklog on long tasks. It helps me keep track of decisions and loose ends as the conversation moves on.
